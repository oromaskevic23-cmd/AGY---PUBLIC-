Privacy and attestation

Principles
- Data minimization: store only necessary data on-chain.
- Attestation: use cryptographic attestations and selective disclosure where required.
- Privacy-preserving verification: leverage zero-knowledge or other privacy techniques when necessary (design patterns only — specifics in spec).

Attestation patterns
- Signed attestations from trusted issuers
- Selective disclosure credentials
- Time-limited proofs and non-replayable evidence

Interoperability and cross-ledger patterns

Principles
- Interoperability via well-defined bridges, attestations, and verification oracles.
- Do not assume token equivalence across systems without explicit design and governance.

Patterns
- Cross-ledger attestations and proofs
- Messaging relays with cryptographic validation
- Bridge operators subject to governance and multi-party security
